
\section{Summary}\label{sec:Summary}

To summarize now this paper.
Congestion control is researched since more than 40 years and there are a lot of developements made.
Most of these are in recent years, because of the increase of data that is send throught networks.
We showed that the algorithms became more and more advanced and that there was even a counter movement where its goal was to make congestion control algorithm more human to understand.

The newest congestion control algorithms are more specialised for their environments like Copa-D for mobile networks or PowerTCP for data center networks.
This however makes it hard to compare with each other, because there behavior will change in a different network or like in PowerTCP's case, they are not even possible anymore.
